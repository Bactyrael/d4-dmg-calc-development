# Unique Charms & Seals

## Andariel's Visage
**Power:** Lucky Hit: Up to a 20% chance to trigger a poison nova that deals 40% of the damage dealt over 5 seconds to enemies in the area.

## Azurewrath
**Power:** After Freeze expires, enemies explode for [100 - 120]% of the damage you dealt to them while Frozen.

## Banished Lord's Talisman
**Power:** After you spend 275 of your Primary Resource, gain 4 stacks of Overpower. Your Critical Strikes deal [8.0 - 10.0]%[x] increased damage per stack of Overpower.

## Blood Moon Breeches
**Power:** Your Summons have a [7.0 - 10.0]% chance to randomly inflict Decrepify or Iron Maiden when they deal damage. You deal [50 - 60]%[x] increased Critical Strike Damage to enemies affected by your Curses.

## Blood-Mad Idol
**Power:** You are always Berserking but take 200%[x] increased damage as Burning over 8 seconds. While Burning, Berserking grants an additional [120 - 150]%[x] increased damage.

## Bloodless Scream
**Power:** Your Darkness Skills Chill enemies for up to 100% and deal [200 - 250]%[x] increased damage to Frozen enemies and bosses. Every 800 times you damage enemies with Darkness Skills, all Nearby enemies are Feared for 0.25 seconds before Freezing for 3 more seconds.

## Crown of Lucion
**Power:** Each time you use a Skill with a Primary Resource Cost, gain [5.0 - 7.5]%[x] increased damage and Resource Cost is increased by 30%[+] for 6 seconds, stacking up to 6 times.

## Doombringer
**Power:** Lucky Hit: Up to a 40% chance to deal 1000% Weapon Damage as Shadow damage to surrounding enemies and reduce their damage dealt by 25% for 5 seconds.

## Endurant Faith
**Power:** When you would be damaged for at least 30% of your Maximum Life at once, it is instead distributed over the next 4 seconds and reduced by [15.0 - 20.0]%.

## Fists of Fate
**Power:** Your attacks randomly deal 1% to [250 - 300]% of their normal damage.

## Flickerstep
**Power:** Each enemy you Evade through reduces your active Ultimate Cooldown by [3.0 - 4.0] seconds, up to 10 seconds.

## Frostburn
**Power:** Lucky Hit: Up to a 50% chance to Freeze enemies for 1 second. You deal [40 - 50]%[x] increased damage to Frozen enemies.

## Godslayer Crown
**Power:** When you attempt to Incapacitate an enemy, you mark them and all surrounding enemies, pulling them in and dealing [7.5 - 10.0]%[x] increased damage to them.

## Gravewalker's Hand
**Power:** Your Essence Generation is increased by [30 - 40]%. Your Bone Skills deal 0.5%[x] increased damage for each point of Essence you have when Cast, up to [60 - 80]%[x].

## Harlequin Crest
**Power:** Gain +6 Rank to all Skills.

## Heir of Perdition
**Power:** Succumb to hatred and earn Mother's Favor. Slaughtering enemies briefly steals 15%[+] Critical Strike Chance from surrounding allies with Mother's Favor. Mother's Favor always grants you 15%[x] increased damage.

## Locran's Talisman
**Power:** Critical Strikes deal [120.0 - 150.0]%[x] increased damage but your Critical Strike Chance is reduced by 50%[+].

## Melted Heart of Selig
**Power:** Damage is dealt to your Primary Resource before Life and damage taken this way is drastically reduced. Your Primary Resource is doubled but Maximum Life is reduced by 75%.

## Mother's Embrace
**Power:** If a Core Skill hits 4 or more enemies, [50 - 60]% of the Resource cost is refunded.

## Omen of Pain
**Power:** A dark aura surrounds you, inflicting Decrepify and Iron Maiden on enemies around you and increasing your damage to Close enemies by [10.0 - 15.0]%[x]. Curses inflicted this way spread to surrounding targets every second.

## Pact of Bone
**Power:** Your Minions gain [30 - 35]%[+] Attack Speed and Critical Strike Chance. When one of your Minions die, your other Minions enrage, dealing [30 - 35]%[x] increased damage for 3 seconds.

## Paingorger's Gauntlets
**Power:** Damaging enemies with a Non-Basic Skill cast marks them for 3 seconds. When a Basic Skill first hits a marked enemy, the Basic Skill's damage is echoed to all marked enemies, dealing [100 - 125]%[x] increased damage.

## Penitent Greaves
**Power:** You are permanently Unhindered and leave behind a trail of frost that Chills enemies. Your Chill effects cause enemies to take [10 - 15]%[x] increased damage from you.

## Rakanoth's Wake
**Power:** When you cast a Skill with a Cooldown, you explode, dealing [300 - 350]% Weapon Damage as Fire damage.

## Razorplate
**Power:** Thorns has a 10% chance to deal [180 - 200]%[x] increased damage.

## Red Blessing
**Power:** While Healthy, gain 2 Maximum Overpower. Blood Orbs grant 2 stacks of Overpower. You deal [8.0 - 10.0]%[x] increased damage per stack of Overpower.

## Ring of Starless Skies
**Power:** Spending your Primary Resource reduces the Resource cost of your Skills and increases your damage by 10%[x] for 3 seconds, up to 50%[x].

## Rustbitten Dirk
**Power:** Deal [90 - 120]%[x] increased damage to isolated enemies.

## Shroud of False Death
**Power:** When you would be dealt fatal damage, instead you Heal to full Life, gain a Barrier for 100% of your Maximum Life for 3 seconds, and Knock Back Close enemies. This power is disabled once used until you return to town.

## Soulbrand
**Power:** Your Healing Potions grant a Barrier for 100% of your Maximum Life for 1 second. You gain [25 - 30]% Damage Reduction while you have a Barrier and may drink your Healing Potion while at full Life.

## Tassets of the Dawning Sky
**Power:** When you take damage from a Non-Physical damage type, you gain [130.0 - 160.0]%[+] Resistance to that damage type for 6 seconds. This effect can only apply to one damage type at a time.

## Temerity
**Power:** Effects that Heal you beyond 100% Life grant you a Barrier up to [80 - 100]% of your Maximum Life that lasts for 8 seconds. You may now drink your Healing Potion while at full Life.

## The Butcher's Cleaver
**Power:** Your attacks Critically Strike Injured Enemies. Killing Elites Fears and Slows nearby enemies by [85 - 95]% for 2 seconds.

## The Gloom Ward
**Power:** Shadow damage infects enemies with Shadowblight for 2 seconds. Every 6th time an enemy receives Shadow damage from you while they are affected by Shadowblight, they take an additional [500.0 - 600.0]%[x] of that damage as Corrupting damage over 4 seconds.

## The Grandfather
**Power:** Increases your Critical Strike Damage by 120%[x].

## Thousand-Eye Reaver
**Power:** Moving grants Ferocity, and Maximum Ferocity is increased by [2 - 4]. Ferocity also increases Movement Speed by [6 - 8]%[+] per stack.

## Tibault's Will
**Power:** You deal [15 - 20]%[x] increased damage and gain 50 Primary Resource Regeneration while Unstoppable and for 5 seconds after.

## Tyrael's Might
**Power:** Gain 20% Damage Reduction. While at full Life, your Skills unleash a divine barrage dealing [360% Weapon Damage].

## Wendigo Brand
**Power:** Every 15 kills in the last [50 - 60] seconds increases your damage by 2%[x], and Maximum Life by 1%[+].

## Will of Rathma
**Power:** Vulnerable, Weakened, Crowd Controlled, or Corrupted enemies are Afflicted, taking 40% increased damage from you. Curse Skills deal [600 - 750]% Weapon Damage as Shadow Damage when applied to Afflicted enemies.

## Wyrdskin
**Power:** Attacks apply Vulnerable to Distant Enemies, and Weakened to Close Enemies. You deal [30 - 40]%[x] increased damage to Enemies that are both Vulnerable and Weakened.

## X'Fal's Corroded Signet
**Power:** Each Damage over Time type you apply explodes every second, dealing [300 - 350]% Weapon Damage as damage over 4 seconds to surrounding enemies.

## Yen's Blessing
**Power:** Casting a Skill has a [40 - 60]% chance to trigger a Non-Mobility, Non-Ultimate Skill that is currently on Cooldown. Can only occur once every 8 seconds.
